## 🧩 About

**Rocket League Overlay** is a comprehensive overlay for Rocket League featuring real-time stats, custom HUD, training tools, boost tracker, and replay analysis.

Based on popular tools like **BakkesMod**, **RL Stats**, and **Rocket League Trainer**.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Features
- Real-time Stats Overlay — display MMR, goals, assists, saves
- Custom HUD — move and resize game elements
- Boost Tracker — monitor boost usage and availability
- Replay Analysis — review past matches with detailed metrics
- Training Tools — custom training packs and drills

### 🎨 Visual Enhancements
- Custom Skins — apply visual modifications (client-side)
- Goal Explosion Effects — customize visual effects
- Ball Trail — enhanced ball visibility

### 🏋️ Practice & Training
- Free Play Enhancer — additional options in free play
- Custom Training Editor — create and share training packs
- Instant Replay Control — slow motion and frame advance
